## Description (2653 characters)

```
Cordia Classroom is an AI study app for college students that turns course material (slides, PDFs, lectures and Canvas pages) into study guides, flashcards and practice tests.

The Chrome extension turns the course page you have open into a study guide, without copying and pasting.

What it reads, in this order:
• Text you selected on the page
• A document embedded in the page: PDF, PowerPoint, Word, Google Docs, Slides or Sheets, text or images
• The page's own text
• A document the page links to, when the page itself has little text
• A screenshot of the visible page, if the page can't be read any other way

How it works:
• Side panel: click the Cordia Classroom icon in the toolbar, or press Ctrl+Shift+Y (Command+Shift+Y on Mac). You can change the shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
• Make study guide: four steps light up (Capture, Read, Focus, Create) and the guide appears in the panel. You can also select text, right-click and choose "Make study guide from selection".
• Save to Classroom: keeps the guide in your Cordia Classroom Library and opens it there.
• Cordia Tutor: ask about the page you're on. It reads the page first if you haven't made a guide yet and remembers the conversation; New chat starts over.

Works on Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle and D2L Brightspace course pages, Canvas file previews, Google Docs, Slides and Sheets, and other web pages.

When it reads the page: only when you click Make study guide, choose "Make study guide from selection", or ask the Tutor a question before a guide exists. Cordia Classroom can also ask it to read a Quizlet set or a YouTube transcript you add there; it reads nothing else for the website.

Plans: Free is $0 a month and includes 3 study guides, 10 tutor prompts, 5 Explain views, 30 AI reading actions, 30 recording minutes and 10 visuals each month, plus one try each of practice sets, AI quizzes, NCLEX sets and Pro quiz sessions. Guides made with the extension count toward the same monthly study guides as the app. Pro is $4.99 a month and Academic Weapon is $9.99 a month. New accounts can try Academic Weapon free for 7 days with no card, once per account.

Privacy: the extension reads a page only when you ask it to, as described above, and sends what it reads only to Cordia Classroom to make your guide or answer your question. Documents are downloaded with your own browser sign-in. It doesn't collect your browsing history, cookies or passwords, and it keeps your Cordia Classroom sign-in in Chrome's extension storage so you stay connected. The full privacy policy is at https://classroom.cordiaai.io/privacy.

Questions or problems: support@cordiaai.io
```
